Frequently Asked Questions

How long does processing take?

Once your documents are complete and your payment is confirmed, our team starts immediately. Timelines depend on the destination country.

How do I pay the processing fee?

After registering, open the Payment tab in your dashboard to see our bank and mobile money details, then submit your transaction reference.

Which documents do I need?

A passport and your academic credentials are required. A passport photo and supporting documents are optional but helpful.

Is my information safe?

Yes. Your files are stored securely and only you and our agency staff can access them. Public verification never shows your email or phone.

Can I get a refund?

Processing fees cover work already carried out. Please contact support to discuss special circumstances.
